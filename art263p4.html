Imprints

a reflection on the marks we leave on the world

Previous Next

What does your texture look like?

"Leave no trace"

"Tread lightly"

"Be considerate of others"

These words often are used as instructions, guidelines on our interactions with the
world. We’re constantly leaving traces – making our marks in the proverbial sand. Some
of our marks stay longer than others, but all will disappear with time.

Whether it be tire tracks, manicured grass, or pebbles on a beach, the human touch is ever-present in our daily lives. Sometimes it’s controlled and intentional, but mostly it’s not.

It’s worthwhile to reflect on our wake, the textures and patterns we leave behind. For everyone they can mean something different – stories of triumph, of loss, and of life. Nature forms around us, and the patterns we leave.

This project was a reflection on our marks on the world. I tried to focus on the little things - minute traces of people, across a variety of landscapes.
