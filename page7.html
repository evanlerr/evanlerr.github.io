Light as Metaphor

intangible traces

Previous Next
Focusing on the little things.

We're constantly moving around our world with our heads down, and ignoring the joy, and the stories that are told around us. 

Light is the strongest storyteller of all, capable of communicating warmth and safety, amid cold and dark. 

This project was a focus on light and how I'm able to manipulate feeling through tone. The project involved manipulation and emphasis on the origin of light sources.
